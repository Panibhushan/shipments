📌 Conditions for Creating Warehouse

  1. Warehouse ID must be in upper case.
  2. Warehouse ID cannot contain any white spaces (spaces, tabs, next-line characters).
  3. Warehouse ID must be at least 3 characters long.
  4. Warehouse ID must be maximum 5 characters only.
  5. Warehouse Name must be at least 5 characters long.
  6. Warehouse Name must be maximum 15 characters only.
  7. Warehouse Address must be at least 10 characters only.
  8. Warehouse Address must be maximum 50 characters only.
  9. Warehouse Status: Active = Shipping is allowed. Disabled = Shipping is not allowed.
