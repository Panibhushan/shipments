📌 Conditions for creating shipment

  1. Select a Customer first.
  2. Enter Item details (Item-Id, UOM, Quantity).
  3. If you want multiple lines, then click on 'Add Line' and if you want to delete a line click on ❌ symbol at the right end of the line.
  4. Click On 'Check Inventory Availability' to get the details of warehouses which has enough inventory to fulfill the shipment.
  5. From the resultant warehouses, select the convenient warehouse and the click on 'Create Shipment'.
