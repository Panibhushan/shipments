📌 Conditions for Creating customer

  1. Customer ID must be in upper case.
  2. Customer ID cannot contain any white spaces (spaces, tabs, next-line characters).
  3. Customer ID must be at least 3 characters long.
  4. Customer ID must be maximum 5 characters only.
  5. Customer Name must be at least 5 characters long.
  6. Customer Name must be maximum 30 characters only.
  7. Valid Up To date must be a future date (earliest is tomorrow).
  8. Customer Status: Active = Shipping is allowed. Disabled = Shipping is not allowed.
