📌 Steps for creating shipment

  1. Select a Customer.
  2. Fill in the Delivery Address. Enter the 6-digit PIN and click Check Delivery to auto-fill District, State, and Country.
  3. Enter Item details (Item ID, UOM, Quantity).
  4. Click Add Line for multiple items. Click ❌ to remove a line.
  5. Each line must have a unique Item + UOM combination.
  6. Click Check Inventory Availability to see eligible warehouses.
  7. Select a warehouse and click Create Shipment.
