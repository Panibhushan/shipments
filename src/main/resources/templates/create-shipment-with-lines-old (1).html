📌 Steps for creating shipment

  1. Select a Customer first.
  2. Enter Item details (Item-Id, UOM, Quantity).
  3. If you want multiple lines, click 'Add Line'. Click ❌ to delete a line.
  4. Note: Shipment can have only one line for unique item-uom combination. You cannot enter the same item-uom combination in multiple lines, it will give error.
  5. Click 'Check Inventory Availability' to see available warehouses.
  6. Select a warehouse from the results, then click 'Create Shipment'.
