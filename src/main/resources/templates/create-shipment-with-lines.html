📋 Getting Started

  1. Pick a Customer.
  2. Expand and fill the Delivery Address. Use "Check Delivery" to auto-fill.
  3. Expand Shipment Lines and add items with Item ID, UOM, and Quantity.
  4. Click Check Inventory to find available warehouses.
  5. Select a warehouse and click Create Shipment.
